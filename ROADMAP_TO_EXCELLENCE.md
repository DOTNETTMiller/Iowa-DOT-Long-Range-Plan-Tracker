# Iowa DOT Interactive Tracker
## Roadmap to Becoming the Preeminent Government Project Transparency Solution

### 🎯 Vision
Transform the Iowa DOT Tracker into the **gold standard** for government transparency and public engagement in transportation infrastructure projects.

**Why:** Show WHERE projects are happening

#### Implementation:

  - Live traffic data overlay

- Planners can identify geographic gaps
