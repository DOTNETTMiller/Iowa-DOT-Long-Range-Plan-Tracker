About the Maintenance Division

The Maintenance Division is responsible for preserving and maintaining Iowa's 9,300 miles of primary road system, 4,100 bridges, rest areas, welcome centers, and other transportation infrastructure. Operating through 6 districts and 100+ maintenance garages statewide, the division ensures safe, reliable travel for all Iowans year-round.
