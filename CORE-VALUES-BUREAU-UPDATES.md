# Core Values & Bureau Updates Addition

## Overview
Adding Iowa DOT's 5 core values and biweekly bureau updates to the modern tracker.

## 5 Core Values
1. **🛡️ Safety First** - Commitment to safety of everyone using the transportation system
2. **👥 People Matter** - Importance of employees, public, and all stakeholders
3. **🎯 Customer Focused** - Excellent service and meeting needs of public and partners
4. **🤝 Servant Leadership** - Leadership focused on serving and supporting others
5. **✓ Integrity Without Exception** - Honesty, accountability, and ethical conduct

## Bureau Biweekly Updates
Tabs for 10 bureaus providing latest progress updates:
1. Program Management
2. Systems Planning
3. Traffic & Safety
4. Bridge Management
5. Modal Transportation
6. Maintenance Division
7. Location & Environment
8. Right of Way
9. Materials
10. New & Emerging Transportation Technologies
